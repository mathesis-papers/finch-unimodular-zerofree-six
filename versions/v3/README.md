# Extending Towse–Campbell's rank-one constructions: a counterexample to Finch's balanced order-six conjecture

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Extending Towse and Campbell's integer rank-one constructions (2016, Section 2, Theorem 1 and Corollaries 1–2) to the zerofree setting, we refute Finch's Conjecture 4 in Small Matrices with Small Inverses: Unimodular Zerofree Cases (arXiv:2605.03691v4, June 12, 2026, Section 5, p. 7) by constructing a positive integral matrix of order six with determinant -1, no zero entry in either it or its inverse, and maximum absolute entry 3 in both. A diagonal sign matrix plus a rank-one matrix gives the construction. Every even order at least two admits such a positive matrix with any prescribed common maximum at least three; every odd order at least three admits one with common maximum three. We also give positive order-six examples with maxima (3,4) and (3,5), and identify the parts of Finch's extremal conjecture covered by his later bounds.
